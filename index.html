01 — about

A little about my process

I'm a UI/UX Designer and Web Developer with hands-on experience in responsive design, content management, and digital marketing. My background is in Computer Science, which means I design with a working knowledge of what's feasible to build — and I like sitting close to development so nothing gets lost in translation.

Most recently, I worked as a UI/UX Designer Intern at Backend Solutions and Technologies Inc., where I designed interfaces for digital payment systems, transaction portals, and administrative platforms — creating wireframes, prototypes, and user flows, then working directly with developers through sprints and QA to get the details right.

Outside of client and internship work, I co-developed Project LiF, a lip-reading learning tool that won Best in Thesis and placed at a national engineering awards competition.
